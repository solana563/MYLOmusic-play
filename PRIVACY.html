Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-01-01

Short version: your imported songs, playlists, likes, saved stations and settings stay on your device. MYLO uses Supabase only to authenticate you and synchronize your chosen username and abstract avatar preset.

Information stored on your device

MYLO stores local audio files, parsed music metadata, downscaled artwork, playlists, liked items, saved stations and preferences in IndexedDB and local storage on your device. This content is not uploaded to MYLO or Supabase.

Account information

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address and authentication credentials. Your selected MYLO username and abstract avatar preset identifier are stored in Supabase Auth user metadata so they can follow your account across signed-in devices. No profile photograph is uploaded; avatars are generated inside the app from preset artwork.

Device music permission

Native Android and iOS builds may request permission to read music already on your device. MYLO uses this access to list and play music locally. It stores lightweight references and metadata rather than duplicating the full device library. You may revoke access at any time in system settings.

Third-party services

  • Supabase: authentication and profile metadata.
  • YouTube: video search and playback.
  • radio-browser.info: station directory and streams.
  • Apple iTunes Search API, TheAudioDB, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive: metadata and artwork.
  • lrclib.net: synchronized and plain lyrics.
  • ipapi.co: approximate country detection for local radio.

Those services receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and browser/app headers when contacted, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications and background playback

If enabled, notifications display playback or sleep-timer status. Background audio and Media Session controls allow playback from the lock screen, Bluetooth devices and notification controls.

Deletion

You can clear the local library from Profile settings and clear app data through your browser or operating system. To request deletion of your authentication account and synced username/avatar metadata, contact the support address listed in the store listing.

Children

MYLO is not directed to children under 13. Third-party radio and video content may be unsuitable for younger users.
